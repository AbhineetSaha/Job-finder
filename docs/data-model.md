# Data model

PostgreSQL 16. Schema defined in `src/db/schema.ts`, migrations generated by
drizzle-kit into `drizzle/` and applied by `src/db/migrate.ts`.

## Design decisions

**Company and contact are separate from prospect.** The brief's §8 field list
mixes company attributes, contact attributes, and pipeline state into one
table. Storing that flat would duplicate company data across every contact at
the same company and make deduplication (§12) unreliable. The normalised form
is:

```
companies (1) ──< contacts (1) ──< prospects (pipeline state, 1:1 with contact)
```

A prospect is *"this contact, being worked"*. The §8 field list is fully
preserved — it is reachable through the join, and the API/CSV export flattens
it back to exactly those field names.

**Normalisation stops there.** `research`, `qualification`, and `deal` are 1:1
with prospect and could be columns, but are separate tables because they have
independent lifecycles, independent audit needs, and (for research) child rows
for sources. Beyond that the schema stays flat — no EAV, no generic
attribute bags (brief §51: *"Avoid unnecessary normalisation if it creates
excessive complexity"*).

**Single-operator, multi-user-ready.** Every business row carries `user_id`.
Today one operator uses the system; the column means authorization is already
row-scoped and a second seat is a policy change, not a migration.

## Tables

### Identity and configuration

**`users`** — `id`, `email` (citext-normalised, unique), `password_hash`
(scrypt, salt embedded), `role` (`OWNER` | `OPERATOR`), `created_at`,
`updated_at`, `disabled_at`.

**`sessions`** — `id`, `user_id`, `token_hash` (SHA-256 of the cookie value;
the raw token is never stored), `expires_at`, `created_at`, `last_used_at`,
`user_agent`, `ip`. Deleting a row revokes immediately.

**`user_profiles`** — 1:1 with user. `name`, `title`, `bio`, `location`,
`timezone`, `email`, `phone`, `portfolio_url`, `github_url`, `linkedin_url`,
`skills` (jsonb string[]), `industries` (jsonb string[]), `hourly_rate`
(numeric), `minimum_project_value` (numeric), `availability`. Nothing about the
operator is hard-coded anywhere in the application (brief §41).

**`settings`** — one row per user. Holds operational configuration that must be
changeable without a deploy: `global_send_paused`, `daily_send_limit`,
`hourly_send_limit`, `per_domain_daily_limit`, `min_delay_seconds`,
`max_delay_seconds`, `default_timezone`, `followup_enabled`,
`qualification_weights` (jsonb), `unsubscribe_footer`, `postal_address`,
`retention_days_*`.

Environment variables provide the *defaults*; the settings row, when populated,
takes precedence, so limits can be tightened at 2am without redeploying.

### Prospecting

**`companies`** — `id`, `user_id`, `name`, `normalized_name`, `domain`,
`normalized_domain`, `website`, `linkedin_url`, `country` (default `US`),
`state`, `city`, `timezone`, `industry`, `company_size`, `funding_stage`,
`description`, `technology_stack` (jsonb string[]), `engineering_signals`
(jsonb string[]), `hiring_signals` (jsonb string[]), `pain_points` (jsonb
string[]), `source`, `source_url`, `created_at`, `updated_at`.

Unique: `(user_id, normalized_domain)` where domain is non-null;
`(user_id, normalized_name)` as a secondary guard.

**`contacts`** — `id`, `user_id`, `company_id`, `first_name`, `last_name`,
`full_name`, `role`, `role_category` (enum, see below), `email`,
`normalized_email`, `linkedin_url`, `timezone`, `created_at`, `updated_at`.

Unique: `(user_id, normalized_email)`.

`role_category`: `FOUNDER`, `CO_FOUNDER`, `CTO`, `VP_ENGINEERING`,
`HEAD_OF_ENGINEERING`, `ENGINEERING_MANAGER`, `TECHNICAL_DECISION_MAKER`,
`OTHER`, `UNKNOWN`. Derived deterministically from the free-text role by
`src/domain/roles.ts` and overridable by hand. `contact_reason` (free text)
records why this person specifically (brief §4).

**`prospects`** — the pipeline row. `id`, `user_id`, `company_id`, `contact_id`
(unique — one live prospect per contact), `status` (enum, §9 of the brief),
`qualification_score`, `qualification_band`, `service_id`, `notes`,
`created_at`, `updated_at`, `last_contacted_at`, `next_follow_up_at`,
`status_changed_at`, `do_not_contact_reason`.

**`qualification_scores`** — `prospect_id`, `score`, `band`, `reasons` (jsonb
array of `{code, label, points, source}`), `weights_version`, `signals` (jsonb
— the exact tri-state inputs used), `computed_at`. Kept as its own table so a
re-score is a new row and the history of *why* a prospect was scored a given
way survives a weights change.

**`research`** — 1:1 with prospect. `company_description`, `product`,
`target_customers`, `technology_stack`, `engineering_team_size`,
`hiring_activity`, `recent_product_activity`, `potential_pain_point`,
`why_relevant`, `why_contacting_them`, `reason_for_reaching_out_now`,
`additional_notes`, `checklist_complete` (generated from the ten §15
questions), `updated_at`.

**`research_sources`** — `id`, `research_id`, `field` (which claim it supports),
`url`, `title`, `note`, `created_at`. URLs are validated to `http`/`https` and
rendered with `rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow"`.

### Offering

**`services`** — `id`, `user_id`, `key`, `name`, `description`, `bullets`
(jsonb string[]), `technologies` (jsonb string[]), `active`, `sort_order`.
Seeded with the five offerings in brief §16, fully editable.

**`portfolio_items`** — `id`, `user_id`, `service_id`, `title`, `description`,
`technologies` (jsonb), `url`, `github_url`, `image_url`, `tags` (jsonb),
`is_public`. The UI carries a standing warning against confidential client
material (brief §42); no field invites private data.

### Outreach

**`templates`** — `id`, `user_id`, `name`, `kind` (`INITIAL`, `FOLLOW_UP`,
`FINAL`), `subject_template`, `body_template`, `required_variables` (jsonb,
derived on save), `active`, `created_at`, `updated_at`.

**`campaigns`** — `id`, `user_id`, `name`, `description`, `service_id`,
`status` (`DRAFT`|`READY`|`RUNNING`|`PAUSED`|`COMPLETED`|`ARCHIVED`),
`target_criteria` (jsonb), `daily_limit`, `hourly_limit`, `timezone`,
`sending_windows` (jsonb array of `{start:"09:00", end:"11:30"}`),
`send_days` (jsonb int[], 1=Mon), `created_at`, `updated_at`, `started_at`,
`paused_at`.

**`campaign_steps`** — `id`, `campaign_id`, `position` (0-based), `delay_days`,
`delay_hours`, `template_id`, `enabled`. Unique `(campaign_id, position)`.
Nothing about the Day 0 / 4 / 9 / 16 default is hard-coded — it is seed data
(brief §26).

**`campaign_members`** — enrolment. `id`, `campaign_id`, `prospect_id`,
`status` (`ACTIVE`|`STOPPED`|`COMPLETED`), `current_position`, `enrolled_at`,
`stopped_at`, `stop_reason` (enum, brief §27). Unique
`(campaign_id, prospect_id)`, plus a partial unique index guaranteeing a
prospect is `ACTIVE` in at most one campaign at a time.

**`messages`** — one per outbound email. `id`, `user_id`, `prospect_id`,
`contact_id`, `campaign_member_id`, `campaign_step_id`, `template_id`,
`direction` (`OUTBOUND`|`INBOUND`), `subject`, `body_text`, `variables` (jsonb
— exactly what was interpolated), `status` (`DRAFT`|`PENDING_APPROVAL`|
`APPROVED`|`SCHEDULED`|`QUEUED`|`SENDING`|`SENT`|`DELIVERED`|`BOUNCED`|
`FAILED`|`BLOCKED`|`CANCELLED`), `content_hash`, `scheduled_at`, `sent_at`,
`provider_message_id`, `blocked_reason`, `created_at`, `updated_at`.

Partial unique index `(campaign_member_id, campaign_step_id)` where both are
non-null — one message per sequence step per enrolment, permanently.

**`message_approvals`** — `id`, `message_id`, `approved_by`, `approved_at`,
`approval_version` (increments per message), `content_hash`, `revoked_at`,
`revoked_reason`. Append-only: an edit inserts a revocation, never a delete.

**`message_attempts`** — `id`, `message_id`, `attempt_number`,
`idempotency_key` (UNIQUE), `status` (`STARTED`|`SUCCEEDED`|`FAILED`|
`BLOCKED`), `provider`, `provider_message_id`, `error_code`, `error_message`,
`started_at`, `finished_at`. Inserted *before* the provider call.

**`replies`** — `id`, `user_id`, `prospect_id`, `contact_id`, `message_id`
(the outbound message replied to, when identifiable), `provider_message_id`,
`from_email`, `subject`, `body_text`, `received_at`, `classification`
(`UNCLASSIFIED`|`POSITIVE`|`INTERESTED`|`QUESTION`|`NOT_INTERESTED`|
`REFERRAL`|`OTHER`), `classified_by`, `classified_at`.

Classification is a human action. Nothing infers it (brief §34).

**`suppression_list`** — `id`, `user_id`, `normalized_email` (nullable),
`normalized_domain` (nullable), `scope` (`EMAIL`|`DOMAIN`), `reason`
(`UNSUBSCRIBED`|`DO_NOT_CONTACT`|`BOUNCED`|`INVALID`|`MANUAL_BLOCK`),
`note`, `created_by`, `created_at`, `removed_at`, `removed_by`,
`removed_reason`.

Unique on `(user_id, scope, normalized_email, normalized_domain)` for live
rows. Suppression survives prospect deletion by design — see
"Retention" below.

**`unsubscribe_tokens`** — `id`, `contact_id`, `token_hash`, `created_at`,
`used_at`. One-click unsubscribe links carry an unguessable token; the raw
token is never stored.

### CRM

**`activities`** — the timeline. `id`, `user_id`, `prospect_id`, `type`,
`title`, `body`, `occurred_at`, `metadata` (jsonb), `created_at`. Every status
transition writes one (brief §9).

**`meetings`** — `id`, `user_id`, `prospect_id`, `scheduled_for` (timestamptz),
`timezone`, `meeting_url`, `notes`, `outcome`, `next_action`, `status`,
`created_at`, `updated_at`.

**`deals`** — `id`, `user_id`, `prospect_id`, `estimated_value` (numeric),
`currency` (default `USD`), `proposal_date`, `expected_close_date`,
`contract_type` (`HOURLY`|`FIXED`|`RETAINER`), `hourly_rate`,
`estimated_hours`, `retainer_value`, `status` (`OPEN`|`WON`|`LOST`), `notes`,
`closed_at`, `created_at`, `updated_at`.

### Infrastructure

**`jobs`** — the queue. `id`, `kind`, `payload` (jsonb), `status`
(`PENDING`|`CLAIMED`|`DONE`|`FAILED`|`CANCELLED`), `run_after`, `attempts`,
`max_attempts`, `claimed_at`, `claimed_by`, `last_error`, `dedupe_key`
(nullable, UNIQUE where non-null), `created_at`, `updated_at`.

**`audit_logs`** — `id`, `user_id` (actor), `actor_type`
(`USER`|`SYSTEM`|`WEBHOOK`), `action`, `entity_type`, `entity_id`, `metadata`
(jsonb), `request_id`, `ip`, `created_at`. No update or delete path exists in
application code; a DB-level revoke of UPDATE/DELETE on this table for the
application role is documented in `docs/operations.md`.

**`webhook_events`** — `id`, `provider`, `provider_event_id` (UNIQUE),
`event_type`, `payload` (jsonb), `signature_verified`, `processed_at`,
`error`, `received_at`. The unique provider event id makes webhook delivery
idempotent and defeats replay.

**`send_ledger`** — `id`, `user_id`, `sent_at`, `recipient_domain`. A narrow
append-only table used for rate-limit counting so limit checks never scan
`messages`. Indexed on `(user_id, sent_at)` and
`(user_id, recipient_domain, sent_at)`.

## Indexes

Added against the actual query patterns, not speculatively:

| Index | Query it serves |
| --- | --- |
| `contacts (user_id, normalized_email)` UNIQUE | dedup on import; suppression join |
| `companies (user_id, normalized_domain)` UNIQUE | dedup on import |
| `companies (user_id, normalized_name)` UNIQUE | dedup fallback |
| `prospects (user_id, status)` | pipeline board, review queue |
| `prospects (user_id, qualification_score DESC)` | prospect list default sort |
| `prospects (user_id, next_follow_up_at)` WHERE not null | "follow-ups due" dashboard card |
| `prospects (user_id, last_contacted_at)` | filters, analytics |
| `prospects (user_id, created_at DESC)` | keyset pagination |
| `messages (user_id, status, scheduled_at)` | scheduler sweep |
| `messages (campaign_member_id, campaign_step_id)` PARTIAL UNIQUE | duplicate-send guard |
| `messages (prospect_id, created_at)` | conversation view |
| `campaign_members (campaign_id, status)` | campaign progress |
| `campaign_members (prospect_id) WHERE status='ACTIVE'` UNIQUE | one active sequence per prospect |
| `jobs (status, run_after)` | queue claim |
| `jobs (dedupe_key) WHERE not null` UNIQUE | enqueue idempotency |
| `suppression_list (user_id, normalized_email) WHERE removed_at IS NULL` | pre-send check |
| `suppression_list (user_id, normalized_domain) WHERE removed_at IS NULL` | pre-send check |
| `send_ledger (user_id, sent_at)` | daily/hourly limits |
| `send_ledger (user_id, recipient_domain, sent_at)` | per-domain limit |
| `activities (prospect_id, occurred_at DESC)` | timeline |
| `audit_logs (entity_type, entity_id, created_at DESC)` | entity audit view |
| `message_attempts (idempotency_key)` UNIQUE | retry safety |
| `webhook_events (provider_event_id)` UNIQUE | webhook replay defence |

## Pagination

Keyset pagination on `(created_at, id)` for prospect, message, and activity
lists. No endpoint offers an unbounded result set; `limit` is clamped
server-side to 100 (brief §50).

## Retention and deletion

Configurable per-user retention in `settings`: `retention_days_activities`,
`retention_days_messages`, `retention_days_webhook_events`. A scheduled job
deletes rows past the horizon.

**The one thing deletion never removes is suppression.** Deleting a prospect,
contact, or company cascades to research, messages, activities, deals and
meetings, but *first* promotes any live suppression to a standalone row keyed
by normalised email/domain with `contact_id` nulled. Otherwise deleting a
contact who unsubscribed would silently make them contactable again — a
compliance failure disguised as a data-hygiene feature. This is enforced in
`src/services/deletion.ts` and covered by
`tests/integration/deletion-preserves-suppression.test.ts`.
